New to the studio

Three easy steps in.

Most people who walk through our door haven't practised in years, or ever. Here is how we suggest you begin.

  1. Take the intro offer

    $45 buys three weeks of unlimited classes, any style. Your three weeks start on the day of your first class, not the day you buy.

  2. Start slow

    Pick a class marked All levels, or join the four-week Foundations course if you want the basics taught properly.

  3. Find your rhythm

    After three weeks you'll know how often you come. Then choose a class pass or membership. We'll help you pick.

Your first visit

What to expect on the day

  • Arrive 10 minutes early so we can show you around and ask about injuries.
  • Mats, blocks and bolsters are free to borrow. Bring water if you like.
  • Wear soft, stretchy clothes. Shoes and phones stay in the cubbies.
  • Showers and towels are free, so you can come before work.
  • Hands-on help is optional. Flip the card by your mat to say no to touch.
  • Bike racks out front; street parking is free after 6 pm.

Classes

Five ways to practise.

Each class tells you how hard it works you and how warm the room is, so you can choose by how you feel today.

  • Breath & movement

    Vinyasa Flow

    Our most popular class. Poses linked to breath in a steady, musical sequence that builds heat and finishes long and slow.

    Good for anyone with a little experience who wants strength and a clearer head. Options are offered for every pose.

    • Effort
    • Room 24°C
    • Length 60 / 75 min
  • Slow & still

    Yin & Restore

    Long, supported holds on bolsters and blankets, held three to five minutes each. Lights low, no music, nothing to prove.

    Good for stiff hips, busy minds and the evening after a hard week. Pairs well with any stronger class.

    • Effort
    • Room 23°C
    • Length 75 min
  • Gently heated

    Warm Flow

    A slower vinyasa in our infrared room at 32°C. Warm enough to soften muscles and work up a sweat, cool enough to stay comfortable.

    Good for regular practitioners who want depth. Bring a towel and drink water beforehand. Not offered during pregnancy.

    • Effort
    • Room 32°C
    • Length 60 min
  • All levels

    Slow Basics

    The fundamentals, taught at walking pace with time for questions. A drop-in version of our Foundations course.

    Good for complete beginners, people returning after injury, and anyone who'd like to slow down and get the details right.

    • Effort
    • Room 24°C
    • Length 60 min
  • Pregnancy

    Prenatal Yoga

    Breath work, gentle strength and positions for comfort, taught by a doula-trained teacher. Tea and conversation afterwards.

    Good from 14 weeks to birth, with or without yoga experience. Partners are welcome at the last Saturday class of each month.

    • Effort
    • Room 23°C
    • Length 60 min

Beginners' course

Foundations, four Sundays.

A small group of ten, the same teacher each week, and the whole thing built for people who've never done a downward dog. You'll leave ready for any All-levels class.

  1. Week one
    Breath and standing

    How to breathe in class, and the standing poses everything else is built on.

  2. Week two
    Sun salutations, slowly

    The sequence at the heart of vinyasa, broken down and pieced back together.

  3. Week three
    Props, twists and backbends

    Using blocks and straps so poses fit your body, not the other way round.

  4. Week four
    Your first full class

    A complete, gentle 75-minute practice, then a chat about where to go next.

$96for 4 sessions, includes a mat to keep
