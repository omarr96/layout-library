FAQ

Good to know

Will servicing here affect my warranty?
No. By law, independent garages can service your car without affecting the manufacturer's warranty, as long as approved parts are used. We always use them.
What if you find something else wrong?
We'll call or text you with photos and a price. Nothing extra is done without your OK.
How long will my car take?
Most services and repairs are done the same day. You can follow every step on our tracking page.
Do you work on electric cars?
Yes. Kev is EV certified, and we service Teslas, hybrids and most other electric cars.
