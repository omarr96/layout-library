FAQ

Before you come

When should I arrive?
Ten minutes early is perfect. It gives you time for tea and a short form about your skin and health.
Can I have a massage while pregnant?
Yes, after your first trimester. We offer a pregnancy massage with special cushions. Please tell us when you book.
What's your cancellation policy?
Free to cancel or move up to 24 hours before. Later than that we charge 50% of the treatment.
Do you have parking?
There's a car park on Rose Lane, two minutes away. We're also a 5-minute walk from Angel station.
Are your products vegan?
All of our skincare is vegan and cruelty free, and most of it is made in small batches in the UK.
