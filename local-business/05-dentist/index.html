FAQ

Common questions

Do you take my insurance?
We accept most PPO plans and file the claim for you. Send us your plan name and we'll check your cover before your visit.
What if I don't have insurance?
Our membership plan covers two cleanings, exams and X-rays for $29/month, plus 20% off other treatment.
I'm very anxious about dentists. Can you help?
Yes. Tell us when you book. We'll go slow, agree a stop signal, and offer sedation options if you'd like them.
At what age should my child first visit?
By their first birthday, or when the first tooth appears. Early visits are short and fun.
