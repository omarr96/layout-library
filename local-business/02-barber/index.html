FAQ

Questions

Do I need to book?
No, walk-ins are always welcome. Booking just means you don't wait. Saturdays get busy, so book if you can.
How much should I tip?
Tips are never expected, but if you're happy, 15–20% is common. You can tip by card.
What if I'm late?
We hold your chair for 10 minutes. After that we may need to move you to the next free slot.
Do you cut curly and textured hair?
Absolutely. All of our barbers are trained in every hair type, and Dre specialises in designs and textured cuts.
