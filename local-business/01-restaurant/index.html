Our story

Everything starts with the fire

Marco and Lina opened Olive & Ember with one oven, eight tables and a simple idea: cook honest food over real fire and let the ingredients speak. Ten years later the oven is still burning every night.

Our vegetables come from three farms within 40 km, our bread is fermented for 48 hours, and our pasta is rolled by hand every morning.

Meet the chef

“Cook less, taste more”

Marco trained in Naples and Lyon before coming home to cook the food he grew up with. His rule is simple: never more than four ingredients on a plate, and every one of them has to earn its place.

Every Thursday he runs a five-course tasting menu at the kitchen counter for just eight guests.

FAQ

Good to know

Can't find your answer? Call us, we're happy to help.

Do you take walk-ins?
Yes. We keep the bar and terrace for walk-ins every night. On Fridays and Saturdays we suggest booking a table inside.
Do you have vegetarian and vegan dishes?
About half of our menu is vegetarian, and the kitchen can make most dishes vegan. Just tell your server.
Can you handle allergies?
Yes. Add your allergies when you book and our chef will plan around them. Our kitchen does handle nuts and gluten.
Do you host private events?
Our private room seats up to 22 guests. We offer set menus from €45 per person with wine pairing.
Is there parking nearby?
The nearest car park is Praça Harbour, a 3-minute walk. Tram 28 stops right outside.
