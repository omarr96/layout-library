FAQ

Before you arrive

What time is check-in and check-out?
Check-in is from 3 pm and check-out by 11 am. Leave your bags with us and enjoy the pool before or after.
Is breakfast included?
Yes, always. It's served in the lemon garden from 7:30 to 11:00, with lots of homemade cakes.
Is there parking?
Positano is tiny, so we have a valet service with a private garage for €35 a day.
Can I cancel?
Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival on flexible rates. Book direct for the best terms.
Is the hotel suitable for children?
Children of all ages are welcome. The garden rooms and Villa Lucia are best for families.
