Before you visit

Do you take walk-ins?
Always. Half the bar is kept for walk-ins every night.
Is there a dress code?
No dress code. Come as you are.
Is it 18+?
Yes, we're over-18s only and may ask for ID.
Can I book the whole bar?
On Mondays, yes. Private hire for up to 60 guests, with your own playlist or band.
