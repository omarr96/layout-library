FAQ

Good questions

I'm a total beginner. Is that okay?
Most of our members started as beginners. Every new member gets a free intro session with a coach.
Is there a joining fee or contract?
No joining fee and no contract on monthly plans. Cancel any time with 30 days' notice.
How does 24/7 access work?
You get a key fob and app pass. Staffed hours are 6 am – 10 pm; outside those, the gym is monitored and has an emergency line.
Can I freeze my membership?
Yes, up to 3 months a year for travel, injury or anything else. Freezing is free.
