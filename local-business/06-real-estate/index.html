FAQ

Buying & selling, explained

How much does it cost to sell with you?
Our fee is a percentage of the sale price, paid only when your home sells. It includes photos, a video tour, staging advice and all the paperwork.
Do I pay anything as a buyer?
In most cases the seller pays the buyer's agent fee. We'll explain exactly how it works for each home before you make an offer.
How long does buying a home take?
From accepted offer to keys usually takes 30–45 days. Finding the right home can be days or months, and we'll go at your pace.
Can you help me rent instead?
Yes. We list rentals too and can help with applications, viewings and the lease.
