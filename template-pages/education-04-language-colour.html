Quick questions

Can I join in the middle of a term?

Yes. Most groups take new students every Monday once your level is checked.

What if the class is too easy or too hard?

Tell your teacher. We will move you to a better group, free of charge.

Do teens and adults learn together?

No. Teens have their own groups, with extra care and parent updates.

Can I switch between in class and online?

Yes, any week. Your course book and progress move with you.
