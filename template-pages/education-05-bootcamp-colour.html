Do I need any coding experience?

No. Most students start from zero. A short free prep course gets everyone to the same starting line before week one.

Can I keep my job while I study?

Yes, on the part-time track. It runs on weekday evenings and Saturdays, at around 20 hours a week.

What happens if I fall behind?

Mentors run daily help sessions, and you can move to a later cohort once at no extra cost.

Is the online course the same as campus?

Same curriculum, same instructors and same live hours. Only the room changes.

Do you guarantee a job?

We do not promise jobs. We give you a coach, mock interviews and introductions to hiring partners until you are hired.

What laptop do I need?

Any laptop from the last few years works. If you do not have one, we can lend you one for the course.
