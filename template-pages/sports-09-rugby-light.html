Our club

Built by members, run by members

Nobody owns this club. It belongs to the people who mark the pitch, wash the kit and coach on cold Sunday mornings.

  1. 01

    Respect

    For team mates, the opposition and the referee, always.

  2. 02

    Teamwork

    Fifteen jobs, one result. Nobody wins a match alone.

  3. 03

    Discipline

    Train properly, play fair and look after the ground.

  4. 04

    Enjoyment

    If it is not fun, we are doing it wrong.

  1. 1934

    Founded

    A handful of friends, one borrowed field and a set of hooped shirts.

  2. 1968

    Clubhouse built

    Members laid the bricks themselves over two summers.

  3. 1996

    Minis begin

    The first Sunday morning session, with nine children.

  4. 2019

    Women take the field

    A new squad, and a cup final in its second season.
