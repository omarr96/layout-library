Questions

Everything applicants ask us.

Talk to the admissions team

Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. We reply to emails within two working days.

017 00000000 example@gmail.com
What grades do I need?

Each course page lists its typical offer. We also look at your personal statement and reference, not only grades.

Can I apply without A levels?

Yes. We accept a wide range of qualifications, and many courses offer a foundation year route.

When will I hear back?

Most applicants hear from us within six weeks. Courses with interviews can take a little longer.

Is accommodation guaranteed?

First-year students who apply for halls by the end of June are guaranteed a room.

Do you offer part-time study?

Many postgraduate courses can be studied part time, and some undergraduate courses too.

Can I visit if I miss an open day?

Yes. Book a self-guided campus tour on most weekdays, or join a virtual tour online.
