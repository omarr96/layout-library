New to volleyball?

Good questions before your first night.

Do I need to have played before?

Not at all. Beginner nights start from the very first touch of the ball, and a coach stays with new members all session.

What should I wear and bring?

Sports clothes, indoor trainers and a water bottle. Knee pads are handy but not needed on day one. For beach, just bring a towel.

How old do children need to be?

Mini volley starts at age six with a lighter ball and a lower net. Parents are welcome to stay and watch.

Is the first session really free?

Yes. Come along, try a full session and only think about joining if you enjoyed it.

Can I play matches straight away?

Social games are open to everyone. League teams are picked by the coaches once you have trained for a few weeks.
