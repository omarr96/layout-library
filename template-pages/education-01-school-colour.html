Admissions

Joining us, step by step.

Most pupils join at ages 4, 7, 11 and 16, and we welcome families at other times when places allow.

  1. I

    Visit us

    Come to an open morning or book a private tour with the admissions team.

    Any time of year
  2. II

    Register

    Complete the short registration form and send it with the registration fee.

    By the deadline
  3. III

    Assessment and meeting

    A relaxed taster day with friendly assessments and a meeting with the head.

    In January
  4. IV

    Offer and acceptance

    We send offers by email. Accept your place with a deposit to confirm.

    From February

Often, yes. Contact the admissions team to check places in the right year group.

Our learning support team works with families and teachers to plan the right help.
