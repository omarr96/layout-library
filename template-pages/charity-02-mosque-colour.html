About the centre

A home for the whole community.

What began as a rented room for a few families is now a centre for prayer, learning and care, open to everyone in the neighbourhood.

The centre is run by volunteers and funded by its members. We keep our doors open every day and our accounts open every year.

New visitors

Visiting for the first time?

You are welcome here. This is what a first visit usually looks like.

  1. 1

    Arrive at the front lobby

    A volunteer at the desk will greet you and answer any question.

  2. 2

    Leave your shoes on the racks

    The halls are carpeted. Chairs are available for anyone who needs one.

  3. 3

    Sit, watch or join in

    You may observe quietly from the back or ask your guide to explain.

Can I visit if I am not a member of the faith?

Yes. Visitors of every background are welcome any day. Come to the front lobby and a volunteer will show you around.

What should I wear?

Modest, comfortable clothing that covers shoulders and knees. Scarves are available at the entrance if you would like one.

Is there a prayer space for women?

Yes. The women's hall has its own entrance, washing area and a quiet room for mothers with small children.

Where can I park?

There is free parking behind the building. It fills quickly on Fridays, so please walk or share a car if you can.

Can I bring children or a group?

Children are always welcome. For school or group visits, send us a message a week ahead so a guide can be ready.
