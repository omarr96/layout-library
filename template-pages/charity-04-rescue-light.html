Good to know

Questions people ask before they adopt

Still unsure? Talk to us.

Our adoption team answers every day, 10am to 4pm.

017 00000000 example@gmail.com
Who can adopt from you?

Adults aged 18 and over with a settled home. If you rent, we just need a note from your landlord.

How long does adoption take?

Most adoptions take about a week from the first visit to the day you take your animal home.

What does the fee cover?

Vaccinations, a microchip, neutering, a health check and four weeks of free advice.

Can I adopt if I have other pets or children?

Often yes. We arrange a calm first meeting so everyone can get to know each other.

What if it does not work out?

Tell us early. We offer behaviour support, and we will always take an animal back.

I found a stray. What should I do?

Keep it safe and warm, then call us. We will check for a microchip and take it from there.
