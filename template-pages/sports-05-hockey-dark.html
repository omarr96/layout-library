Learn to play

An eight-week block for children and adults who have never played. We lend you all the kit.

  • Skates, stick, helmet and pads on loan
  • One coach for every six beginners
  • Sunday afternoons, 45 minutes
  • A place in a team when you finish

Game day

Your night at the rink

Bring a jumper, bring your voice. Here is what to know before the puck drops.

  • Doors and warm-up

    Doors one hour before the start. Warm-up is worth seeing.

  • Parking

    Free at the rink. It fills up early on Saturdays.

  • Family stand

    Quiet seats near the benches. Ear defenders to borrow.

  • Food and drink

    Hot drinks and snacks at the kiosk on both floors.
