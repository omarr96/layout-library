What to expect on a first visit

Plan your visit

Your first Sunday, minute by minute.

Walking into a new church can feel like a lot. Here is exactly what happens, so there are no surprises.

Tell us you are coming

We will save you a seat and meet you at the door.

  • Come as you are
  • Children welcome
  • Step-free access
  1. 10:40

    Arrive and park

    Park for free behind the building. Someone from the welcome team will be at the main door.

  2. 10:45

    Coffee and a hello

    Grab a drink, check children into their groups and find a seat anywhere you like.

  3. 11:00

    The service

    About an hour: songs with the words on screen, a short talk and a time of prayer. Join in as much or as little as you like.

    Around 60 minutes
  4. 12:05

    Stay for lunch

    There is always something to eat afterwards. Nobody will put you on the spot.

A word from our pastor

You do not need to have it all together to walk through our doors.

Whether you have been part of a church all your life or have never set foot in one, there is room for you here. We are a mix of ages and backgrounds, learning to follow the same hope together.

Come with your questions. Sit at the back if you like. I would love to say hello on Sunday.
