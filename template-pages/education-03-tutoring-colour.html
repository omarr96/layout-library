How it works

From first hello to real progress

Four simple steps, and you are kept in the loop at every one of them.

  1. 1

    Step one

    Free assessment

    A relaxed chat and short tasks show us where your child is and how they like to learn.

    Free · about 45 minutes

  2. 2

    Step two

    A learning plan

    We set clear goals with you and choose group, one-to-one or online lessons to fit.

    Shared within two days

  3. 3

    Step three

    Weekly lessons

    Small, friendly lessons with the same tutor, plus short homework to keep things moving.

    After school or Saturday

  4. 4

    Step four

    Progress reports

    Every half term you get a written report and a quick call about what comes next.

    Every six weeks

Questions parents ask

What happens at the free assessment?

Your child does a few short, friendly tasks with a tutor while you have a chat with us. It takes about 45 minutes and there is no test pressure.

How big are the group classes?

Never more than six pupils, grouped by age and level so everyone gets time with the tutor.

Can we switch between centre and online?

Yes. Many families mix the two, for example online in winter and in centre the rest of the year.

Do you set homework?

A short task each week, usually 15 to 20 minutes, that follows on from the lesson.

How do I pay and can I cancel?

Fees are paid monthly. You can pause or stop with two weeks' notice, with no fee to leave.
