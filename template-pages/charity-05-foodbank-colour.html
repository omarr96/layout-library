Get help

Getting food here is simple.

Everyone hits a hard week. This is how a visit works, start to finish.

  1. 1

    Come along

    Walk in any time we are open. You do not need a voucher, a referral or an appointment.

    No booking
  2. 2

    Have a quick chat

    A volunteer says hello and asks how many people you are feeding and what you cannot eat.

    About 5 minutes
  3. 3

    Choose your food

    Pick what your household will really eat from the shelves, like a small shop. Then come back whenever you need to.

    Around 3 days of food

Getting food

Do I need a referral or a voucher?

No. Anyone who lives locally can walk in when we are open. We ask your first name and how many people you are feeding, and that is all.

How often can I come?

As often as you need to. Most people visit once a week for a while, then less as things get easier.

Will anyone find out I came?

No. We never share names with anyone, and the entrance is at the side of the hall, away from the main road.

Giving and volunteering

Is there anything you cannot accept?

Opened packets, food past its use-by date, home-made food and alcohol. Everything else is welcome, including toiletries and pet food.

Where does my money go?

Almost all of it goes straight on food. The rest keeps the lights on and the van running. Our accounts are open for anyone to read.

Can young people or work teams volunteer?

Yes. Volunteers aged 16 and over can sign up alone, younger helpers come with an adult, and we host a work team most Fridays.
