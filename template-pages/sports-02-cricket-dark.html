Club history

One ground, one pavilion and well over a century of summers.

The club began with a borrowed field and eleven neighbours. The field is ours now, and the neighbours number in the hundreds.

Generations of the same families have opened the batting here, made the teas and rolled the square. We look after that heritage while making the club an easy place for new players to walk into.
