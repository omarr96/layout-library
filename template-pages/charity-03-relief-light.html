Questions about giving

Straight answers before you give

Supporter care

A real person answers, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

How much of my gift reaches people in need?

86% of every gift goes to our programmes. The rest raises the next gift and keeps the charity safe and well run.

Can I choose where my gift is used?

Yes. Give to a named appeal and it is spent on that appeal. General gifts go wherever the need is greatest.

Can I change or stop a monthly gift?

At any time. Call or email supporter care and we will update it the same day.

Will I get a receipt for tax purposes?

We email a receipt after every gift and a yearly summary for monthly supporters.

How do I know the aid arrives?

Field teams report from each project, and independent auditors check our accounts every year.
