// FAQ

Questions

Does Lumen record without people knowing?
Never. Lumen always announces itself or shows a recording notice to everyone in the call.
Is my data used to train AI?
No. Your meetings are yours. They're encrypted and never used for training.
Which apps does it work with?
Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and in-person meetings, plus Slack, Notion, Jira, Asana, Linear and HubSpot.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, in two clicks. You keep your notes on the Free plan.
