Why it matters

One connected blue world.

From sunlit coral reefs to the pitch-black deep, every layer of the sea depends on the others. Plankton at the surface feed whales; falling "marine snow" feeds creatures that have never seen light.

When one layer suffers, the whole system feels it — and so do we.
