Our method

From seed to self-sustaining forest

We don't plant and leave. Every tree is cared for until the forest can look after itself.

  1. 🌰
    Months 0–2

    Seed collection

    Local seed keepers gather native species from nearby mother trees.

  2. 🌱
    Months 2–8

    Community nursery

    Seedlings grow strong in nurseries run by village cooperatives.

  3. 🌿
    Year 1

    Planting season

    Planted with the first rains, mixed to mimic a natural forest.

  4. 🌳
    Years 2–5

    Watch & protect

    Satellite checks, fire breaks and paid rangers until canopy closes.

Questions

Good questions deserve honest answers

Tip: press Tab to move between questions and Enter to open one.

How do I know my tree was really planted?

Every tree is tagged to a planting site with GPS coordinates. You'll receive geotagged photos at planting, then seasonal satellite updates for five years.

Where does my €1 actually go?

€0.62 pays local planters and rangers, €0.21 covers seedlings and nurseries, €0.10 funds monitoring, and €0.07 runs Canopy.

Our audited accounts are published every April.

Can trees really offset my footprint?

Trees help, but they're not a licence to pollute. We encourage reducing emissions first — and planting for climate, biodiversity and communities together.

Can my company partner with you?

Yes. We offer per-product planting, employee forests and verified impact reports. Write to partners@canopy.example.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

In the EU, UK and US, yes. You'll receive a receipt automatically after each donation.
