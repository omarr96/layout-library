物語 Our story

Cooking is listening to the season.

"I learned in Osaka that the fish tells you what it wants. My job is only to get out of the way — a little salt, a little fire, and rice at the temperature of skin."

We buy from three small boats and one farmer. When they have nothing, we have less on the menu. That's how it should be.

— Kenji Morita, head chef
Kenji M.
