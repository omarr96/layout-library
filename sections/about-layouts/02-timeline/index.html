Since 1979

Forty-five years of making things to outlast us.

From a single lathe in a rented cowshed to a 30-person workshop in the Teme Valley, Holt & Ash has grown slowly, on purpose. Every piece is still marked with the initials of the maker who finished it.

  1. 1979

    A lathe in a cowshed

    Walter Holt leaves his apprenticeship and starts turning chair legs for local farmhouses, paid partly in firewood.

    The beginning
  2. 1988

    The Teme stool

    Our three-legged ash stool is picked up by a Hereford gallery. Thirty-six years on, it's still our best-seller.

    First design
  3. 2003

    Ash joins Holt

    Designer Priya Ashworth becomes partner, bringing the moulded-seat, oak-legged dining chair that got us into 200 homes a year.

    Partnership
  4. 2016

    The Ludlow showroom

    We open a converted granary as a showroom and start running weekend joinery classes for beginners.

    New home
  5. Today

    Thirty makers, one forest

    We now plant two trees in our own Shropshire woodland for every one we use, and offer a lifetime repair promise.

    Carbon positive
