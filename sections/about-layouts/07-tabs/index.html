Why we exist

Make good companies measurably easier to run

Most of our clients are 50 to 2,000 people, profitable, and stretched. Our mission is to give them the operating discipline of a much bigger business without the bureaucracy that usually comes with it.

  • Every engagement has three numbers we commit to in writing
  • Part of our fee is held back until those numbers move
