Our story

Three generations, one very stubborn sourdough starter.

Marlowe & Daughters began in 1987 in the back kitchen of a terraced house on Quarry Lane, with a wood-fired oven Grandad Tom built from salvaged firebrick.

Today his daughter Ruth and granddaughters Esme and Nell run the bakehouse — but the starter is the same one Tom fed every night at ten. We still mill our rye on site, ferment every loaf for at least 36 hours, and light the oven at 3:30 every morning so the first tray of buns is out before the school run.

No shortcuts, no improvers, nothing you can't pronounce. Just flour, water, salt, time — and a family that argues lovingly about crumb.
