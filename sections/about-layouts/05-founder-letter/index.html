A letter from our founder

Skincare that asks less of your skin, not more.

Dear friend,

For most of my twenties, my bathroom shelf looked like a chemistry set. Twelve steps, forty ingredients, and skin that was red and tight by lunchtime. When a dermatologist finally told me to stop almost everything, my skin calmed down within a month.

That's where Maren began — at my kitchen table in Stroud in 2017, with a secondhand double boiler and a notebook full of failed batches. I wanted a few honest products that did one thing well, made from plants I could name and growers I could visit.

"If an ingredient doesn't earn its place, it doesn't go in the bottle."

Seven years later, we're a team of fourteen. Every formula still has fewer than ten ingredients, is tested on real people (never animals), and is poured in small batches so it reaches you fresh. Our jars are glass, our refills are free to return, and our calendula comes from a farm twenty minutes up the road.

Thank you for trusting us with something as personal as your skin. I read every note you send — please keep them coming.

With warmth,

Annika Maren
Annika Maren · Founder, Maren Botanics
