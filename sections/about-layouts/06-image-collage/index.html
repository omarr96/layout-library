About the house

A merchant's home, kept slowly for guests

Casa Albarrán began as my grandmother's townhouse on Calle Tenorio, three streets from the gorge. In 1998 we opened six rooms; today there are twenty-two, and the fig tree in the patio is still the oldest guest.

We restored the original tiles by hand, kept the cool thick walls, and turned the old stables into a dining room where Inés cooks with what the valley sends that morning. No conference suites, no lobby screens — just good beds, quiet rooms and a terrace for the long evening light.
