(02) About the studio

Malmö / CopenhagenEst. 2006

We are fourteen architects who believe a building should outlast its brief, use less than it needs, and still feel like somewhere you'd choose to spend a Sunday.

Oskar Lindqvist Arkitekter designs schools, libraries and housing across southern Scandinavia. We work mostly in timber and reclaimed brick, and we stay on as advisers for two years after handover to see how people actually use what we drew.

No signature style — just a habit of asking what a place will need in fifty years.
