Our mission

We rescue good food before it's wasted and get it onto the tables that need it — with dignity, not handouts.

What we stand for

Common Table has partnered with 310 community kitchens, schools and pantries since 2014. These four values decide every partnership, every hire and every pound we spend.

  • Dignity first

    Our pantries work like shops — people choose what they take. No forms at the door, no queues on the street.

    92% of visitors say they feel respected

  • Nothing wasted

    What people can't eat goes to animal feed or compost. Last year, less than 1% of donated food went to landfill.

    4,800 tonnes saved in 2024

  • Led locally

    Each hub is run by people from the neighbourhood it serves. We fund, train and get out of the way.

    38 hubs, 38 local leads

  • Open books

    Every donation and every penny we spend is published quarterly. 88p of each £1 goes straight to food and delivery.

    Audited annually since 2016
