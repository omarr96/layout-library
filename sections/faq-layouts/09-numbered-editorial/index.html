Questions, answered

Before we begin

The things clients usually ask in our first conversation — about fees, timelines, planning and how we work with builders.

  1. We visit your home for around 90 minutes, walk the site with you and talk through how you live, what isn't working and what you'd love to change. We'll also give an honest view on budget and whether planning is likely.

    Within a week you'll receive a short written proposal with scope, fees and a programme.

    Cost
    Free within zones 1–3
    Elsewhere
    £350, deducted from fees

  2. For full architectural service we charge a percentage of the construction cost, usually 12–15% depending on complexity. Each RIBA stage is invoiced on completion, so you only commit to one stage at a time.

    Smaller studies and interiors-only projects are quoted as fixed fees.

    Full service
    12–15% of build
    Billing
    Per RIBA stage

  3. For a rear extension with a new kitchen, allow around 14–18 months from first meeting to moving back in: three to four months of design, eight to ten weeks for planning, two months of technical drawings and tender, then five to seven months on site.

    Design
    3–4 months
    On site
    5–7 months

  4. Many single-storey extensions and loft conversions fall under permitted development, which needs only a Lawful Development Certificate. In conservation areas, or for anything at the front of a house, full planning is usually needed. We prepare and submit every application and handle conversations with the council's officers.

    Council decision
    8 weeks (target)
    Approval rate
    94% since 2016

  5. Yes. We tender each project to three or four contractors we've worked with before, compare the prices line by line, and recommend one. During the build we act as contract administrator: fortnightly site visits, valuations and signing off each payment before you make it.

    Tenders
    3–4 builders
    Site visits
    Every 2 weeks

  6. We do, from a single kitchen to a whole house. Our interiors team handles joinery design, lighting, materials and furniture, and orders through our trade accounts — savings we pass straight on to you.

    Fee
    Fixed, from £9,500
    Trade savings
    Passed on in full

  7. To give a project the attention it deserves, we work on builds with a construction budget of £150,000 or more, and interiors projects from £60,000. For smaller ideas we offer a paid two-hour design consultation with sketches you can take to a builder.

    Architecture
    From £150k build
    Consultation
    £480, 2 hours

  8. Two of our architects are certified Passivhaus designers. We start with fabric first — insulation, airtightness and orientation — then heat pumps and solar where they make sense. We also measure embodied carbon on every project and favour reclaimed brick, lime and timber.

    Certified
    Passivhaus designers
    Reported
    Embodied carbon
