Weddings at Wrenfield

Everything you'd like to know

A 17th-century threshing barn, two acres of orchard and walled garden, forty minutes from Bath. Here's what couples ask us most.

The Threshing Barn seats 160 for dinner on long banquet tables, or 140 on rounds. For the evening reception we can welcome up to 220 guests across the barn and courtyard.

Smaller weddings from 30 guests are welcome on Sunday to Thursday dates.

Yes. The Orchard Lawn is licensed for legal ceremonies from April to September, seating up to 150. The registrar fee is set by the local council (currently £620 on a Saturday) and booked by you directly.

We decide together by 10am on the day. The covered Garden Pavilion is set up as a back-up for every outdoor ceremony at no extra cost, and we keep 60 golf umbrellas by the door.

We work with three approved caterers who know the barn kitchen well — a seasonal farm-to-table menu, a wood-fired feast and a vegan kitchen. Three-course menus start from £68 per guest, including staff and tableware.

Absolutely. Any florist and stylist is welcome. Set-up starts from 9am on the day (or 4pm the day before for Saturday weddings). We just ask for no confetti cannons, glitter or naked flames other than in glass holders.

You can supply your own wine and fizz for the drinks reception and dinner with a corkage fee of £12 per bottle, which covers chilling, glassware and service. The evening bar is run by our own team.

Music runs until midnight, the bar closes at 11:45pm and carriages are at 12:30am. Live bands are welcome; we have a sound limiter set to 96 dB to keep the neighbours friendly.

The Coach House has 8 bedrooms sleeping 18, and the bridal suite is included with every full-day booking. The rest are £165 a night with breakfast. There are four more B&Bs within a 5-minute drive.

After a viewing we'll hold your date for 7 days. A £2,500 deposit secures it, with the balance due 12 weeks before. Venue hire starts at £6,900 for midweek dates and £11,500 for a peak-season Saturday.
