Hotel Olivar · Sintra

Before you arrive

Everything guests usually ask us before their stay. For anything else, our front desk is here day and night.

  • What time is check-in and check-out?

    Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is by 11am. Early check-in from 11am or late check-out until 2pm is €35, subject to availability.

  • Is breakfast included?

    Yes, on every room rate. Breakfast is served in the orangery from 7:30 to 10:30am, with pastries from our bakery, local cheeses and eggs cooked to order.

  • Do you have parking?

    We have 14 free spaces in our gated courtyard, assigned on arrival, and two EV chargers. Valet parking is €15 per night if the courtyard is full.

  • Can you arrange an airport transfer?

    Our driver collects from Lisbon Airport for €65 each way for up to 3 guests. The drive takes about 35 minutes. Book at least 24 hours ahead.

  • Are dogs welcome?

    Well-behaved dogs up to 20 kg are welcome in our garden rooms for €25 per night, with a bed, bowls and treats waiting. Please let us know when you book.

  • What is your cancellation policy?

    Flexible rates can be cancelled free of charge up to 7 days before arrival; after that we charge the first night. Advance rates are 15% cheaper but non-refundable.

  • Is there a spa and pool?

    Our heated outdoor pool is open 8am–8pm from April to October. The spa has a sauna and two treatment rooms; massages start at €70 for 50 minutes.

  • Can we bring children?

    Children of all ages are welcome. Cots are free, and an extra bed for children up to 12 is €30 per night including breakfast. Our junior suites sleep a family of four.

  • Do you serve dinner?

    Our restaurant, Casa Olivar, serves a seasonal Portuguese menu Tuesday to Saturday from 7pm. Hotel guests get priority tables; we recommend booking a day ahead.
