    Quotes & buying

    Your price is held for 30 days as long as your details don't change. Saved quotes live in your account and you can buy with one tap from the reminder email.

    Car cover

    You can add up to 4 named drivers in the app. Adding someone with 3+ years' experience usually costs between £0 and £40 a year; a learner or newly qualified driver costs more.

    On Comprehensive and Comprehensive Plus, yes — a small hatchback while yours is fixed at one of our approved garages. Comprehensive Plus also gives you a hire car for up to 21 days if yours is stolen or written off.

    All car policies include the legal minimum cover in the EU. Comprehensive policies include full cover for trips of up to 90 days a year at no extra cost — just tell us before you go.

    Home cover

    It's optional on Standard (about £3 a month) and included on Home Plus. It covers one-off accidents like red wine on the carpet or a drill through a pipe — not wear and tear.

    Add Personal Possessions cover from £4 a month. Any single item worth over £2,000 needs to be listed by name, with a receipt or valuation.

    Making a claim

    Start a claim in the app or call 0330 123 4567, 24 hours a day. Have photos, the date and a rough description ready. You'll get a named handler within one working day.

    Simple claims like a windscreen or a burst pipe are usually settled in 5–10 working days. Larger claims involving loss adjusters or third parties take 4–8 weeks, and we update you at least weekly.

    Your excess is the amount you pay towards each claim. Car policies have a £250 compulsory excess plus any voluntary amount you chose; home policies default to £100. It's shown on your policy schedule.

    Payments & renewals
