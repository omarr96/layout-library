Everything you need to know about Taskloom plans, billing and keeping your team's work safe.

You get every Business feature for 14 days with up to 25 teammates — no credit card needed. When the trial ends you can pick a plan or drop to the Free plan; nothing you created is deleted.

Free covers up to 5 members and 3 projects. Pro is $9 per member per month, and Business is $16 per member per month, both billed annually. Monthly billing costs 20% more. Enterprise pricing is quoted for teams over 250 seats.

Yes. New seats are prorated to the day and appear on your next invoice. Removed seats become a credit on your account rather than a refund, and the credit is applied automatically at renewal.

Our one-click importers bring across boards, tasks, assignees, due dates, comments and attachments. Most workspaces under 10,000 tasks finish importing in under 5 minutes. You can also upload any CSV and map the columns yourself.

Slack, Microsoft Teams, GitHub, GitLab, Figma, Google Drive, Zoom and 60+ more ship out of the box. Everything else connects through Zapier, Make or our REST API and webhooks, which are included on every paid plan.

Taskloom is SOC 2 Type II audited and GDPR compliant. Data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit, and backed up every hour to a separate region. Business plans add SAML SSO, SCIM provisioning and audit logs.
