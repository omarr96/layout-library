Frequently asked

Quick answers on orders, delivery, returns and payment. Pick a topic below.

You have 1 hour after checkout to edit sizes, the address or cancel, straight from the link in your confirmation email. After that the order is packed at our atelier and can no longer be changed — but you can return it free once it arrives.

Each product page lists garment measurements in cm and inches, plus the height and size our model is wearing. Our tailoring runs true to size; knitwear is cut relaxed, so size down if you prefer a closer fit. Still unsure? Our stylists reply on live chat within 5 minutes, 9am–7pm CET.

Core pieces like the Ines trench and the Lune knit are restocked every 6–8 weeks. Seasonal pieces are made in limited runs and rarely return. Tap Notify me on any size to get an email the moment it's back.

Orders placed before 2pm CET ship the same day. Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days in the EU and UK, 4–6 to the US. Express arrives next business day in the EU and in 2 days to the US and UK.

Standard shipping is free on orders over €150 and €7.50 below that. Express costs €18 in the EU and €25 to the UK and US. Every parcel ships carbon-neutral in recycled, tissue-wrapped packaging.

No surprises: for the UK, US, Canada and Switzerland, duties and taxes are calculated at checkout and paid upfront, so nothing is due on delivery. Orders within the EU carry no duties at all.

You can return unworn pieces with tags attached within 30 days of delivery. Returns are free in the EU and UK; US returns cost $8, deducted from your refund. Earrings and swimwear without the hygiene seal can't be returned.

Go to Account → Orders, choose the items and print the prepaid label or show the QR code at any drop-off point. Guest checkout? Use the returns portal with your order number and email.

We refund within 3 business days of your parcel reaching our warehouse, to the original payment method. Your bank may take a further 3–5 days to show it. Prefer an exchange? Choose it in the portal and we'll ship the new size as soon as your return is scanned.

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, iDEAL and Bancontact. All payments are processed securely and your card details are never stored on our servers.
