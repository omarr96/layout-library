Why teams switch to Tallyboard

Everything your projects need. Nothing they don't.

Six tools that replace the spreadsheet, the status meeting and the 40-message thread asking who's on what this week.

    Kanban, list or table — switch views in one click. Custom stages, WIP limits and swimlanes for every team.

    Drag a task and every blocked task shifts with it. See the critical path before the deadline sees you.

    "When status is Review, assign Priya and set due in 2 days." Build rules in plain sentences, not flowcharts.

    Capacity bars per person show who's at 140% before burnout does. Rebalance by dragging tasks between names.

    Briefs, specs and meeting notes live inside the project. Turn any line into a task with a forward slash.

  • Reports that write themselves

    Weekly status goes out every Friday at 4pm — progress, blockers and slipped dates, pulled straight from the board.
