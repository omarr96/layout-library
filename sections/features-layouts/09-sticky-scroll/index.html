How the club works

From a tiny farm lot to your first sip, in five steps.

Slowpour Club roasts single-origin beans to order and ships them while they are still at their peak. You set the taste — we handle the rest.

1Taste profile
  1. 01 2 minutes

    Tell us how you drink it

    A six-question quiz maps your palate — fruity or chocolatey, black or with oat milk, French press or espresso.

    Bright & floralNutty & smoothDark & bold
  2. 02 Every month

    We buy direct from 31 farms

    Micro-lots from Huila, Sidama and Nyeri, paid on average 2.4× the Fairtrade minimum — with the farmer named on every bag.

  3. 03 Every Tuesday

    Roasted to order, never stockpiled

    Our 15-kilo drum roaster only fires once your order is in. Pick whole bean or one of seven grind sizes matched to your brewer.

  4. 04 Within 48 hours

    Letterbox-friendly delivery

    Compostable valve bags slip through the door. Skip, pause or swap origins any time from a single text message.

  5. 05 Every cup

    Rate it and we fine-tune

    Thumbs up or down on each bag and the next one moves closer to your perfect cup. Most members hit it by bag three.
