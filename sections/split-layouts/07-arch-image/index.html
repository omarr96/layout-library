Wedding flowers, Cotswolds

Flowers that look like they were gathered, never arranged.

Every wedding we take on begins in our cutting garden outside Bibury, where we grow over ninety varieties of roses, sweet peas, cosmos and trailing herbs. We design around what is blooming that week — so your bouquet carries the scent and colour of the exact season you said “I do.”

We take just twenty-two weddings a year, which means one designer sees you from first sketch to the final buttonhole.
