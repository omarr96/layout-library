Har gow so thin you can read the menu through them

Jade Steamer's dumpling team pleats each har gow with fourteen folds, and you can taste every one. The prawns snap, the wrappers barely hold.

Go on a weekday morning, take the trolley's first lap seriously, and save room for the custard tarts, which come out of the oven every twenty minutes.

Best dim sum in the city, three years running

Weekend queues start at 9:40. The siu mai and the turnip cake are non-negotiable; the rest of the trolley is a pleasant gamble.

Our inspectors returned four times over the year and found the standard unwavering. Service is brisk, the tea refills are relentless, and prices remain kind.

Cover story8 Nov 2025

Fifty years, two families, one bamboo steamer

The Leung and Ho families have shared one kitchen since 1975. The recipes have barely changed; the queue has only grown.

Upstairs, the old mah-jong room is now a private dining space for twelve. Book it for a banquet and the kitchen will roast a whole suckling pig to order.

Listeners' pick for Sunday yum cha

More than 3,000 listeners voted, and Jade Steamer topped the list by a landslide. The cheung fun got its own round of applause.

Presenter Dina Osei broadcast live from the dining room, interrupted only by the clatter of the trolleys and one very loud birthday table.

The vegetarian dim sum menu nobody talks about

Ask for the green card menu: mushroom cheung fun, chive dumplings and a crispy tofu skin roll that converts meat eaters.

Allergen information is printed on every card, and the team cheerfully adapts dishes for gluten-free guests with a day's notice.
