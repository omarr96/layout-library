Ceviche clásico de corvina

El de la casa · since 2009

Our founder Rosa Quispe grew up above her uncle’s stall in a Lima fish market. She opened Limón with one rule borrowed from him: the fish is never cut before you order, and the lime is never squeezed before the fish is cut.

“If it sits, it cooks. If it cooks, it’s not ceviche anymore.”
Rosa Quispe, chef & owner

What’s on the plate — tap to explore
