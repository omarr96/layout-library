Open kitchen

From the field to the pass in five honest steps

At Fieldstone there’s no wall between you and the cooks. Here’s what happens to a plate before it reaches your table — most of it you can watch from the chef’s bench.

  1. 016:00am

    Source

    Produce arrives from three farms within 40 km. Whatever looks best decides the menu.

  2. 0210:00am

    Prep

    Stocks on, herbs picked, bread proofed. Every knife cut happens in-house.

  3. 035:30pm

    Fire

    The oak hearth is lit at noon and runs all service — no gas on the grill.

  4. 047:00pm

    Plate

    Plated by the cook who made it, finished with leaves from our rooftop beds.

  5. 057:05pm

    Pass

    Head chef Imogen Hale checks every plate before it crosses to your table.
