Nuestra historia

A bar for staying late and ordering one more plate

Taberna Lumbre began as a twelve-stool counter run by two cousins from Valencia, Inés and Álvaro Marín, who missed the noisy, generous tapas bars of home.

Thirteen years on, we still cook over a single charcoal grill, pour vermouth from the barrel and let the table decide how the night goes. Nothing is plated for one — everything is meant to be passed around.

  • Everything cooked on one charcoal grill, called la lumbre
  • Kitchen open until 00:30, Thursday to Saturday
  • 32 Spanish wines by the glass, vermouth on tap
