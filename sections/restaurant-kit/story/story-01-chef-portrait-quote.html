The chef behind the counter

I don’t cook to impress you. I cook so that the fish, the rice and the season can speak a little louder than I do.

Haruto Mizuno Head chef & owner, Kappo Tsukiyo

Haruto trained for eleven years in small kappo kitchens before opening Tsukiyo’s eight-seat cypress counter in 2016. Every evening he cooks directly in front of his guests, and the menu changes with whatever arrives from the morning market.

He still sharpens his knives by hand before every service, ages his own fish for up to nine days, and writes each night’s menu with a brush on washi paper. Ask him about the rice — it’s polished in-house twice a week.

Outside the kitchen he teaches a monthly knife class for young cooks and keeps a small vegetable plot that supplies the shiso and mitsuba you’ll find on your plate.
