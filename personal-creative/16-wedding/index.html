Questions & answers

What should I wear?
Black tie optional for Saturday. Think long dresses, suits and dancing shoes. The garden has gravel paths, so skip stilettos.
Can I bring my kids?
We love your little ones, but Saturday is adults only. We can recommend local babysitters.
What will the weather be like?
Warm days around 26°C and cool evenings in the hills. Bring a light layer.
Can I post photos?
After the ceremony, yes! Use #AmaraAndLeo. During the ceremony we'd love an unplugged moment.
