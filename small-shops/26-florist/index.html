FAQ

Good to know

Where do you deliver?
All BS postcodes by bike or electric van. Further afield, we can send plants and gifts by post.
Can I choose the exact flowers?
We use what's freshest each day, so your bouquet may vary slightly, but always in the same colours and style.
Do you do weddings and events?
Yes! Send us your date and ideas on WhatsApp and we'll book a free consultation.
How do I pay?
We send a secure card payment link once your order is confirmed. You can also pay in store.
