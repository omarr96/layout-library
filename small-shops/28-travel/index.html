FAQ

Before you go

Are flights included?
Tours start and end in the destination, so you can use your own miles. We're happy to book flights for you too.
Can I travel solo?
About 40% of our travellers come alone. Choose a shared room to skip the single supplement.
What if I need to cancel?
Free changes up to 60 days before departure, and full refunds if we ever cancel a trip.
How fit do I need to be?
Each tour has an activity level from 1 to 5. Most are 2 or 3: a few hours of walking a day.
