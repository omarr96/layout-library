❓ FAQ

Questions

Do you have gluten-free options?
Yes, the brownies and almond cake are gluten-free. My kitchen does use flour, so I can't promise zero cross-contact.
How do I pay?
Cash, Venmo or card on delivery or pickup. Custom cakes need a 50% deposit.
Can I order for a big event?
For more than 50 people, message me two weeks ahead and I'll put together a menu.
