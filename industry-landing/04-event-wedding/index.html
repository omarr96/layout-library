How full planning works

Twelve months, calmly paced

Every couple gets a shared planning portal, a fixed meeting schedule and one lead planner from first call to farewell brunch. Here is what the year looks like.

i12 months out

Vision & budget

A two-hour design session, a realistic budget split and a guest-count range.

ii10 months out

Venue & vendors

Three venue tours, then a shortlist of photographers, caterers and florists we trust.

iii6 months out

Design & details

Mood board, tastings, stationery and rentals locked in. Invitations go out.

iv6 weeks out

Run sheet

Final numbers, seating, a timed run sheet shared with every vendor.

vThe day

You just arrive

Two planners on site from load-in to the last car home. You never see a problem.

Questions

Good to know

Still wondering? Call the studio on (845) 555-0142.

How far ahead should we book?
Full planning works best with 12 months, partial with 6–9, and day-of coordination with at least 8 weeks. Peak Saturdays from June to October usually go 14 months ahead.
Do you travel outside the Hudson Valley?
Yes. We plan regularly in New York City, the Berkshires and Connecticut. Destination events are quoted with travel at cost and no extra markup.
How does payment work?
A 30% retainer secures your date, then the balance is split into monthly instalments ending four weeks before the event. You pay vendors directly, so you always see exactly what things cost.
Can we use our own vendors?
Of course. We will happily work with anyone you love, and we will flag it kindly if a contract needs a closer look.
What happens if it rains?
Every outdoor event gets a written wet-weather plan, priced and on hold, agreed with you at the 6-week meeting. We make the call 48 hours out so you don't have to.
