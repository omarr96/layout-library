FAQ

Parents ask us

Will my child have to repeat a school year?
Usually not. Most Dutch, Belgian and German schools recognise an exchange year if the student takes comparable subjects. We send your school a course plan in advance and help with the paperwork on return.
Can we choose the city or region?
For Canada, Australia, the UK and private US schools, yes. For US public schools on a J-1 visa, the placement is assigned, but you can add a region choice for €950.
What if it doesn't work out with the host family?
It happens to around 1 in 12 students, usually for small reasons. Your coordinator mediates first, and if needed we move you to a new family nearby without changing schools.
What does insurance cover?
Health, accident, liability and repatriation, with no excess for doctor visits. Sports injuries are covered, including skiing in Canada. Phones and laptops need separate cover.
When do we pay?
€500 on acceptance, 40% once the host family is confirmed and the rest 8 weeks before departure. Or spread it over 10 monthly payments at no extra cost.
