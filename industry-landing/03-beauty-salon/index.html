07 Good to know

Questions answered

What's your cancellation policy?
Free cancellation or rescheduling up to 24 hours before. Later than that, or no-shows, are charged 50% of the service.
Do I need a patch test?
Yes for first-time colour (48 hours before) and lash or brow tints (24 hours before). It takes five minutes and is free.
Do you work with curly and textured hair?
Absolutely. Jess and Maya are trained in curl cutting and silk presses, and we stock products for every texture.
Can I bring my kids?
Kids' cuts (under 12) are $35 with Jess on weekday afternoons. For longer services we suggest coming solo so you can relax.
