FAQ

Good to know

Still wondering? Call reception on 0117 496 0300.

Do I need to register before an appointment?
No. You can book straight away and register at the same time using the form above. In an emergency, just call and come in.
Do you work with pet insurance?
Yes. We can claim directly from most UK insurers for bills over £250, so you only pay your excess. For smaller bills you pay us and we fill in the claim form for free.
Can I split the cost of bigger treatment?
For treatment over £500 we offer 0% interest over 6 months, subject to a quick credit check. Wellness Club members get the option on bills from £300.
My dog is nervous at the vet. What can you do?
Book a "happy visit": a free 10-minute stop for treats and a fuss, with no needles. We can also prescribe calming medication before visits and see you in a quiet room by the side door.
