    FAQ

    Straight answers

    Will servicing here affect my manufacturer warranty?
    No. Under UK block exemption rules, any VAT-registered garage can service your car during the warranty, as long as they follow the manufacturer schedule and use OE-quality parts. We do both and stamp your book or update the digital record.
    What if you find extra work?
    You get a video and a written price by text. Nothing is done until you reply yes. Advisory items go on a free reminder so you can budget for them.
    How does the courtesy car work?
    Any job over £150 includes a free loan car (a 2023 Toyota Yaris Hybrid). You need a full UK licence held for 2+ years. Fuel back at the same level, please.
    Do you work on electric cars?
    Yes. Ravi and Tom hold IMI high-voltage qualifications, and we have a dedicated insulated EV bay. We service Tesla, Kia, Hyundai, VW ID, Polestar and more.
