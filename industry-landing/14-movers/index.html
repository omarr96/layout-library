FAQ

Before you book

Still unsure? Call Rachel or Sam on the moves desk. They've done this a few thousand times.

How accurate is the instant estimate?
For standard homes it lands within 10% of the final quote about 9 times in 10. The survey firms it up: we check access, parking, stairs and anything unusually heavy, then fix the price in writing.
What if my completion date moves?
Chains slip. We move your date free with 72 hours' notice, and if completion is delayed on the day we can hold your load overnight in our depot at no charge.
Is there anything you won't move?
Paint, gas bottles, petrol and other flammables, plus plants on international moves. Cash, jewellery and passports should travel with you. We'll send a full list with your quote.
How do I pay?
A 15% deposit by card or bank transfer to secure the date, and the balance within 48 hours after delivery. Office moves can be invoiced on 14-day terms.
Do I need to empty drawers and wardrobes?
Clothes can stay on the rail: we bring wardrobe cartons on the day. Drawers should be emptied of anything breakable or heavy, but folded clothes can usually stay put.
