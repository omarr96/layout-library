  • Neck stiffness

Sports injuries

From weekend trail runners to high-school soccer players. Load management and strength, so you return stronger, not just pain-free.

  • Runner's knee
  • Ankle sprains
  • Hamstring strains
  • Achilles tendinopathy
  • Tennis & golfer's elbow
  • Shoulder impingement

Post-surgery rehab

We follow your surgeon's protocol to the week and send them progress notes. Hydrotherapy available at our Milwaukie partner pool.

  • ACL reconstruction
  • Knee & hip replacement
  • Rotator cuff repair
  • Spinal fusion
  • Wrist & hand fractures
  • Ankle ORIF
⏱Typical plan: 2× weekly for 8–16 weeks

Headaches & jaw pain

Many headaches start in the neck. Many jaw clicks start with clenching. Gentle manual therapy and habit work, with your dentist in the loop.

  • Cervicogenic headaches
  • TMJ / jaw pain
  • Dizziness after whiplash
  • Clenching & grinding
  • Concussion recovery

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Do I need a doctor's referral?
Not in Oregon. You can book physical therapy and chiropractic care directly. A few insurance plans still require one for coverage, and we'll check that for you before your first visit.
Physical therapist or chiropractor: who should I see?
Either can assess you, and they work as one team, so you'll be passed across if the other is a better fit. As a rough guide: post-surgery and sports rehab start with a PT; neck pain and headaches often start with a chiropractor.
Will the adjustments hurt?
No. You may hear a pop, which is just gas in the joint. If you'd rather not, we use instrument-assisted or low-force techniques instead. You can say stop at any point.
How quickly can I be seen?
Usually within 3 working days, often the same week. Cancellations go to our waitlist by text, so join it if the slot you want is taken.
What's your cancellation policy?
Please give 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged $50, waived once a year because life happens.
