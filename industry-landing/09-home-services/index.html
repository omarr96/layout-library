FAQ

Good to know

Do you charge to come out?
No trip fee inside our service area. HVAC diagnostics are $89, and that's waived if you go ahead with the repair. Emergency calls at night, on weekends or holidays add $79.
Are your technicians licensed?
Yes. Our electricians and HVAC techs hold Ohio contractor licenses, plumbers are licensed through the City of Columbus, and every tech is background-checked and drug-tested. We carry $2M general liability insurance.
What if the job costs more than the flat rate?
It can't. The flat rate is agreed before work starts. If we find a second problem, we quote it separately and you decide whether to go ahead.
Do you offer financing?
For jobs over $1,000 we offer 12 months same-as-cash financing, subject to approval. Ask your tech for the QR code, it takes about two minutes.
