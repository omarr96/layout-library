Questions families ask

Nothing is a silly question

Or speak to Fiona, our care manager, on 01632 960 427.

How quickly can care start?
Usually within five days of the assessment, and within 24–48 hours for urgent hospital discharges when we have a carer free nearby. We'll always be honest if we can't cover a time.
What if Mum doesn't get on with her carer?
Tell us and we'll change it, with no awkwardness. We match on personality and interests as well as skills, so it rarely happens, but it's your loved one's home and their choice.
Can carers give medication?
Yes. All carers are trained and assessed in medication support, from reminders to giving tablets from blister packs, eye drops and creams. Everything is recorded on the electronic medication chart you can see in the family app.
Do you help with funding?
We'll explain the options during the assessment: council funding after a care needs assessment, direct payments, Attendance Allowance and NHS Continuing Healthcare. We work with North Yorkshire Council and can invoice them directly.
