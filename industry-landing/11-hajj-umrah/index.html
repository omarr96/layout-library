Questions

Often asked

What vaccinations do I need?
The meningitis ACWY vaccine is required for Hajj and recommended for Umrah, and must be given at least 10 days before travel. We'll send a checklist with your booking. Ask your GP or a travel clinic about flu and COVID boosters too.
Can women travel without a mahram?
Saudi rules now allow women to perform Umrah without a mahram. Many sisters still prefer company, so we run women-only groups with a female guide and arrange room-sharing with other sisters.
How does booking Hajj work from the UK?
UK residents apply for Hajj through the official Nusuk Hajj platform. We help you register and prepare, and once places are confirmed you travel with our escorted group, scholars and ground team.
What happens if my visa is refused?
Your deposit and any instalments are refunded in full. This has happened to fewer than 1 in 200 of our pilgrims.
Is my money protected?
Yes. All flight-inclusive packages are ATOL protected and you'll receive an ATOL certificate when you pay your deposit. If we couldn't deliver your trip, you would get your money back or be brought home.
