We think the best rooms look like they took years to gather, even when they took twelve weeks. That means fewer, better things, honest materials, and space left for your own story.

Atelier Verde was founded by Elena Moreau after a decade at architecture firms in Paris and New York. Today we're a team of six designers and a project manager working from a former print shop on Union Street.

We source from small workshops, estate sales and trade-only suppliers, and we track every purchase in a shared budget so there are no surprises. Around 60% of what we specify is vintage, reclaimed or made within 200 miles of the studio.

Questions

Before we begin

How is the design fee calculated?
Full-service projects have a flat design fee based on scope, agreed before we start. Furniture and finishes are billed at our trade price plus a 15% procurement fee, usually less than retail.
Do you work outside New York City?
Full-service projects are within about two hours of Brooklyn, including the Hudson Valley, Connecticut and northern New Jersey. E-design and video consults are available anywhere in the US.
Can you work with furniture we already own?
Absolutely, and we encourage it. We'll photograph and measure what you have and design around the pieces you love, reupholstering or refinishing where it helps.
Do you manage contractors?
Yes. We can bring our trusted builders or coordinate with yours, and we attend site meetings weekly during construction.
How long does a typical project take?
A single room usually takes 8–12 weeks from first meeting to install. Whole-home renovations run 6–12 months depending on permits and custom pieces.
