FAQ

Good questions

Is solar worth it in Yorkshire?
Yes. A south-facing roof in Leeds generates around 880 kWh per kWp each year, about 85% of what the same panels would make in Cornwall. Panels work on bright cloudy days too, just less.
Do I need planning permission?
Usually not. Solar on most homes and heat pumps meeting the noise rules are permitted development. Listed buildings and conservation areas need a check, and we handle that application for you.
Who gets the £7,500 heat pump grant?
Homeowners and small landlords in England and Wales replacing a fossil-fuel boiler, with a valid EPC and no outstanding loft or cavity insulation recommendations. We apply and the grant comes off your invoice.
What happens to power I don't use?
It's exported to the grid and your supplier pays you for it under the Smart Export Guarantee. Rates range from about 4p to 15p per kWh depending on the tariff, and we'll help you pick one.
How long do the panels last?
Panels are guaranteed to still produce at least 87% of their rated output after 25 years, and often keep going well beyond 30. Inverters typically need replacing once, around year 12–15.
