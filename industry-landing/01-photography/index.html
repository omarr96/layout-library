08 / FAQ

Good to know

Do you travel outside Oregon?
Yes. About a third of our work is out of state or abroad. Travel within 100 miles of Portland is included; beyond that we bill flights and lodging at cost, with no day-rate markup.
How do we hold our date?
A signed agreement and a 30% retainer secure your date. The balance is due two weeks before the shoot. We never double-book.
Can we get the raw footage or RAW files?
Raw footage is available as a $600 add-on, delivered on a drive. We don't release unedited RAW photos, but every usable frame is edited and delivered.
What music do you use in films?
Fully licensed tracks from Musicbed and Artlist, chosen with you, so your film can be posted anywhere without being muted.
Who owns the images for brand work?
You get a two-year commercial licence for web, social and print as standard. Buyouts and paid-media extensions are quoted per project.
