FAQ

Before you buckle up

How many lessons will I need?
The DVSA average is around 45 hours of lessons plus 22 hours of private practice. Our pupils average 38 hours. After your first lesson your instructor will give you an honest estimate.
Manual or automatic: which should I learn?
An automatic licence only lets you drive automatics, but most new cars and all electric cars are automatic now. Learners usually need around 25% fewer hours in an automatic. If you might drive a manual van or older family car, learn manual.
Can I use your car for my test?
Yes. Test-day hire is £80 (free with a 20-hour block) and includes a one-hour warm-up lesson right before the test.
What if I need to cancel a lesson?
Move or cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before in the app. Inside 48 hours the lesson is charged in full, unless your instructor can fill the slot.
Is an intensive course right for me?
Intensives suit people who have passed their theory and can clear a full week. Complete beginners should allow at least 30 hours. We'll do a free 1-hour assessment drive first so we don't book a test you're not ready for.
