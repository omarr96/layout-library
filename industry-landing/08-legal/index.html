FAQ

Fair questions

Is the first consultation really free?
Yes. The 20-minute case review is free with no obligation. If you'd like a longer meeting with detailed written advice, that's a fixed £195 + VAT, taken off your fee if you instruct us.
What does "fixed fee" actually cover?
Everything in the scope letter we send before you agree. If the matter changes (for example, an uncontested divorce becomes contested), we tell you in writing and quote again before doing more work.
Do you offer no-win, no-fee?
For many employment tribunal claims, yes, through a damages-based agreement capped at 25% of what you recover, including VAT. We'll tell you if your case qualifies during the free review.
Can we meet in person?
Of course. Our office is at 3 Hardman Street, Spinningfields, open Monday to Friday 8:30–18:00, with evening appointments until 20:00 on Thursdays. Most clients choose video calls.
Is legal aid available?
We don't hold a legal aid contract, but if you may qualify we'll point you to a firm that does, free of charge.
