FAQ

Straight answers

Can't see your question? Call 0117 496 0381, weekdays 8:30–18:00.

What if my business needs more than the package covers?
We'll tell you before doing any extra work and quote a fixed price. Most clients never pay anything beyond their monthly fee; the most common extra is an HMRC enquiry, which our £8/month fee protection add-on covers.
Is there a minimum contract?
Monthly plans roll month to month with 30 days' notice. Annual plans are paid up front, include two months free and a free company formation, and are refundable pro rata if we haven't filed your return yet.
Do I have to use your software?
No. We work with all the major cloud bookkeeping tools. If you don't have one yet, Ledgerkit is included free on the Sole trader plan and costs £12/month on the others.
Does Making Tax Digital for Income Tax affect me?
If your self-employed and property income is over £50,000, quarterly digital updates started in April 2026. The threshold drops to £30,000 from April 2027. Our Sole trader plan includes every quarterly update and the final declaration.
