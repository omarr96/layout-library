Questions

Things parents ask

Can't see yours? Email hello@bramblewoodnursery.co.uk.

How does settling in work?
Three free sessions over two weeks: an hour together, two hours with you nearby, then a morning on their own. Your key person visits your home first if you'd like, and we never rush it.
What happens if my child is unwell?
We follow UK Health Security Agency exclusion guidance. If your child becomes poorly at nursery we'll call you and keep them comfortable with their key person until you arrive. We can give prescribed medicine with a signed form.
How much notice do I need to give?
One calendar month to reduce days or leave. A £100 registration fee secures your place, and £200 deposit is refunded on your final invoice.
Do you close for holidays?
Only bank holidays and the week between Christmas and New Year. We're open all through school holidays, and run a holiday club for Bramblewood graduates up to age 8.
Can you support children with additional needs?
Yes. Priya, our SENCo, works with you, your health visitor and Leeds inclusion services to plan support, and we can apply for additional funding on your behalf.
