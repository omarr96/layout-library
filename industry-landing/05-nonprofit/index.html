Our work

Water that keeps flowing

Building a well is the easy part. Half of rural water points across sub-Saharan Africa break within a few years. Our model pairs every build with local repair crews and sensors, so the water never stops.

Borehole wells

Deep, solar-pumped wells serving 300–1,200 people each, sited with the community.

$9,800 per well

Rainwater tanks

20,000-litre tanks for schools and clinics where groundwater is salty or too deep.

$3,200 per tank

School WASH

Latrines, hand-washing stations and hygiene clubs that keep girls in class.

$5,400 per school

Local repair crews

Trained, paid mechanics on motorbikes who fix 94% of breakdowns within 48 hours.

$120 trains one mechanic
Stories from the field

Real people, real water

Mzimba, Malawi

Grace used to walk 6 km for water. Now she walks to school.

Since the Chikangawa borehole opened in March, school attendance among girls in the village rose from 61% to 89%.

Gulu, Uganda

A rainwater tank ended the dry-season cholera scare at Pabbo Primary.

740 pupils, two tanks and a hygiene club run by twelve-year-olds who take soap very seriously.

Kitui, Kenya

Joseph fixed 212 pumps last year, on a motorbike we bought him.

Our mechanics are paid by small community fees, so repairs never wait for a donor again.

Kasungu, Malawi

Diarrhoea cases at the local clinic fell by two thirds in a year.

Health workers credit the new water point and the hand-washing stations beside it.

Lira, Uganda

The day the water came, nobody went home dry.

Every opening ends the same way: a village-wide splash, and then a meeting about who keeps the key.

“We publish every project's cost and GPS location, and the sensor data that shows it is still working.”Ruth Mbeki, Executive Director

Questions

Straight answers

Can't find it here? Email our supporter team at care@clearwaterfund.org or call (312) 555-0187.

Is my donation tax-deductible?
Yes. Clearwater Fund is a registered 501(c)(3), EIN 47-3318290. You'll get a receipt by email straight away and an annual summary every January.
How do I know the water point still works?
Every pump has a small sensor that reports daily water flow. If it drops, a local mechanic is dispatched. You can see live status for every project on our map.
Why do you need monthly donors?
Monthly gifts let us plan drilling seasons a year ahead and fund the repair crews that keep old wells running. They are the most powerful gift you can give.
Can I change or cancel my monthly gift?
Anytime, in two clicks from your donor account or by replying to any of our emails. No phone calls, no guilt.
Do you sell or share my data?
Never. We don't swap or sell supporter lists, and we only email you about our own work.
