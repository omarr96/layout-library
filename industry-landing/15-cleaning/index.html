FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to supply cleaning products?
No. Your cleaner brings a full kit: plant-based products, microfibre cloths, a HEPA vacuum and a steam mop. If you'd prefer we use your products or a particular brand, just leave a note on your booking.
Will I get the same cleaner every time?
Yes, for weekly and fortnightly cleans. If they're on holiday or off sick we'll text you the name of their cover by 6pm the evening before, and you can choose to skip that visit free.
What's the deposit-back guarantee?
If your landlord or agent withholds deposit over cleaning on anything in our end-of-tenancy checklist, send us the report within 72 hours of the inspection and we'll return to re-clean those items free.
What if something gets broken?
Tell us within 48 hours. We're insured up to €6.5 million and settle most breakage claims within 10 working days, without you needing to go through your home insurance.
How do I cancel or skip a clean?
In the app or by text, free with 48 hours' notice. Inside 48 hours we charge 50% of the visit, because your cleaner has kept that slot for you.
