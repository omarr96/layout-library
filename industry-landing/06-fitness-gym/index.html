Our story

From timber yard to training floor

Ironbark started in a leaky corner of the old Sydney Road timber yard with four racks and twelve founding members. Six years and one very big renovation later, it's 1,400 m² of light, turf and iron, and still run by the same people.

FAQ

Before you come in

Still unsure? Drop in any time 7am–7pm and say hi at the front desk.

I've never been to a gym. Will I fit in?
Absolutely. Around a third of our members had never trained before. Every class is scaled, and your free pass includes a one-on-one welcome with a coach.
Are there joining fees or contracts?
No joining fee and no lock-in on monthly plans. Cancel with 14 days' notice from the app. Yearly plans are paid upfront and can be paused.
How does childminding work?
Our creche runs Monday to Friday, 8:30–11:30am, for kids aged 6 months to 5 years. It's free with Unlimited and Coached, and $8 a session with Open Gym.
Is there parking?
Yes, 40 free spaces behind the building off Albion Street, plus bike racks inside. Anstey station is a 4-minute walk.
Can I freeze my membership?
Up to 8 weeks a year for travel, injury or life, at no cost. Longer freezes are $5 a week.
