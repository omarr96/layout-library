SINCE 1985 Since 1985: 41 years on the same corner

Same griddle. Same booths. Same Lou’s.

In the spring of 1985, Louisa “Lou” Brennan bought a shuttered sandwich shop with her savings and a second-hand flat-top grill. She opened with nine stools, a pie case and a promise: breakfast all day, nobody leaves hungry.

Her daughter Dee runs the place now, but the grill is the original, the booths are the ones Lou re-upholstered in cherry vinyl in 1991, and the burger still gets smashed twice and toasted on a buttered bun.
