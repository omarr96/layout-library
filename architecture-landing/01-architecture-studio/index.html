Our approach

We begin with how you live, not with a style.

Before we draw anything we spend time in the house with you — where the morning light falls, where the bags get dropped, which room nobody uses. The design comes out of that.

  • i.

    Light before square footage

    A well-lit room of 150 sq ft often feels larger than a dim one twice the size. We plan for sun first.

  • ii.

    Keep what is good

    Old floors, solid walls, a fine staircase. Re-using them is cheaper, greener and gives a home its character.

  • iii.

    Drawn to your budget

    We price as we design, with a cost consultant at each stage, so there is no painful surprise at tender.

How a project runs

Five stages, one point of contact.

For a typical extension, about eight to twelve months from first visit to moving back in. You can stop after any stage and keep everything we have drawn.

  1. 01

    First consultation

    We visit, listen, look at the site and talk honestly about budget and what is possible.

    1 visit · 90 min
  2. 02

    Brief & survey

    A measured survey, a written brief and a fixed fee proposal for the stages ahead.

    2–3 weeks
  3. 03

    Design & permits

    Sketches, models and 3D views until it feels right, then we prepare and submit the planning application.

    2–4 months
  4. 04

    Technical & tender

    Construction drawings, specifications and building permits; we help you choose a contractor.

    6–10 weeks
  5. 05

    On site & handover

    Regular site visits, contract administration and a snagging walk-round before you move in.

    4–8 months
