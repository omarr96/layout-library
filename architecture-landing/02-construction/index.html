How a build works

Five stages. No surprises between them.

Here's a typical new home. Every stage ends with a sign-off from you before we spend money on the next one.

  1. Stage 01

    Site visit & brief

    We walk the site with you, check access, soil and services, and agree what you want to build and roughly what it will cost.

    Week 1–2 · free
  2. Stage 02

    Design & fixed price

    Drawings, engineering and a line-by-line estimate. You get one fixed contract price before anything is built.

    Weeks 3–10
  3. Stage 03

    Permits & scheduling

    We lodge the building permit, book inspections and lock in trades and materials against a dated programme.

    Weeks 8–16
  4. Stage 04

    Build

    One site manager from slab to finish. A photo report every Friday and a site walk with you at each milestone.

    6–10 months
  5. Stage 05

    Handover & warranty

    Final inspection, a walk-through, keys and a manual for your building. We come back at 3 and 12 months.

    10-year warranty
