Emergency? Start here

Three things to do while we're on the way.

Call us first — we'll stay on the line and talk you through it. Most damage happens in the first twenty minutes, and these steps stop most of it.

  1. Turn off the water at the mainTurn the shut-off valve clockwise (or the lever across the pipe) until it stops.
  2. Switch off the water heater and any nearby powerIf water is near sockets or the heater, turn them off at the breaker. Don't touch anything wet.
  3. Open the taps and catch what you canDrain the system through the lowest tap, move valuables, and take a few photos for insurance.
