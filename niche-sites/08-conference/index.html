Why Common Ground

Fewer slides about the future. More of what worked.

Every talk is a real project: a street that was closed to cars, a housing block that stayed affordable, a square that people now actually sit in. Speakers show the budget, the politics and the mistakes — not just the renders.

One room, no parallel tracks, long breaks and a walking tour on the second afternoon. You leave with ideas you can take to your own council meeting on Monday.

Getting there

From the airport — 35 min by metroRed line to Alameda, green line to Cais do Sodré, then a seven-minute walk along the river. A taxi takes about 25 minutes.
By train — 4 min walkCais do Sodré station has trains from Cascais and the riverside ferries from Cacilhas and Seixal.
By bikeCovered bike parking for 120 bikes at the entrance. The riverside path runs right past the door.
