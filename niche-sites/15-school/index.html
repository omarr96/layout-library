Welcome from the Head

Big enough to stretch them, small enough to care.

When families visit, they usually tell us the same thing on the way out: it felt calm, and the children looked happy. That's not an accident.

We keep Fernhollow deliberately small so that teachers know each child well, from the four-year-old learning to hold a pencil to the sixteen-year-old sitting their exams. Children learn best when they feel safe, so we start there, and then we ask a lot of them.

The best way to understand us is to come and see a normal working day. I'd be very glad to show you around.

Helen Okafor Mrs Helen Okafor

Curriculum & learning

One school, three stages of growing up.

The same teachers, the same values and the same front gate from Reception to Year 11, so there's no big jump at seven or eleven.

Reception to Year 2: learning through doing

Mornings are for phonics, number and talk; afternoons are for exploring. Every class spends one full morning a week in the woods, whatever the weather.

  • Daily phonics in small groups of six to eight
  • Weekly forest school with a qualified leader
  • A teacher and a teaching assistant in every class
  • Gentle settling-in visits before the first day

Years 3 to 6: confidence and good habits

Class teachers lead English, maths and topic work, while specialists teach science, French, music, art and sport from Year 3, in the senior school's labs and studios.

  • Specialist teaching in five subjects from age seven
  • Weekly swimming at the town pool, Years 3 to 5
  • A residential trip each summer from Year 4
  • No entrance test to move up to the senior school

Years 7 to 11: stretch, support and good exams

Pupils take nine or ten subjects at 16, chosen with their tutor and parents. Sets stay small enough that no one can hide at the back, and no one gets left behind.

  • A personal tutor who meets each pupil every week
  • Triple science, two languages, art, drama and computing
  • Study support and clinics after school, free of charge
  • Careers guidance and a sixth-form choices evening

Admissions

Joining us is simpler than you think.

Most children join in Reception, Year 3 or Year 7, but we welcome families at any point in the year when a place is free.

  1. VisitCome to an open morning or book a private tour on a normal school day.
  2. RegisterComplete a short form online with a $150 registration fee, refunded if no place is offered.
  3. Taster dayYour child spends a day with their future class. For Year 3 and up, a relaxed English and maths check.
  4. OfferWe'll call you within a week, then confirm the place in writing with a deposit.

Parents' guide

The practical questions, answered.

Can't see what you need? The school office is open from 8:00 to 4:30 and is always happy to help.

Navy jumper with the crest, white or pale blue polo shirt, and grey trousers, skirt or pinafore. Only the jumper, PE top and book bag carry the crest; everything else can come from any shop.

Our parent-run second-hand shop opens every Friday after school, and most items cost $3–8.

Two school minibus routes run from Hartley Green and Ashby Mill each morning and after clubs, at $4 a journey. Linden Cross station is a ten-minute walk, and staff meet the 8:22 train.

There's a one-way drop-off loop on Orchard Lane from 8:15, so you don't need to park.

Lunch is cooked every day by our own kitchen team and is included in the fees. There's always a vegetarian main, a salad bar and fruit. Menus run on a three-week cycle and are sent home each term.

We're a nut-free school. Our cook meets every family with a food allergy before their child starts.

Breakfast club runs from 8:00 ($6) and after-school care until 5:45 ($12 a session, including tea). You can book by the session through the parent app, up to 9:00 am on the day.

Our learning support team of three works with children who have dyslexia, dyspraxia, ADHD or need a little extra help for a while. Short-term small-group support is free. One-to-one sessions are charged at cost, and we'll always talk it through with you first.

A short newsletter every Friday, two parents' evenings a year and written reports each summer. Class teachers are at the gate at the end of every day, and reply to emails within one working day.
