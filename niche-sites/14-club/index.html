About the club

A proper club, with a kettle always on.

Millbeck Rovers began with one Sunday side and a borrowed set of goalposts. Fifty years on we have 22 teams, two grass pitches, a floodlit small-sided pitch and a clubhouse that smells of toast every Saturday morning.

We are a not-for-profit club and every coach is a volunteer. Every coach has a background check and coaching training. We care more about kids enjoying it than about results, and we keep fees low so nobody misses out.

“Jonah came along to one taster and now he won't miss a Saturday. I ended up helping run the tea hut.”

Priya Nandakumar, U9 parent (fictional)
Teams & sections

There's a team for you, at any age.

Pick your section, then come to a free taster. The coaches will help you find the right group.

4–8years

Mini Kickers

Games-based sessions on Saturday mornings. No scores and no pressure. Parents stay pitch-side, and many end up joining in.

U9–U16boys & mixed

Juniors

Fourteen teams playing in the local youth league, from 7-a-side up to 11-a-side. One training night a week plus matches on weekends.

5–16+girls & women

Wildcats, Girls & Women

Girls' teams from U10 to U14 and a women's side that welcomes complete beginners. Nobody here minds if you've never played before.

16+men's & walking

Adults & Walking Football

Saturday First XI, Sunday XI and a relaxed reserves squad. Walking football (no running, no tackling) for over-50s and women 30+.

Clubhouse & ground

Kestrel Lane, our home.

We lease the ground from the town council and look after it ourselves. Volunteers cut the grass, mark the lines and keep the clubhouse running.

  • PitchesTwo full-size grass pitches and a floodlit 3G pitch for winter evenings
  • ClubhouseFour changing rooms, showers, first-aid room and defibrillator
  • Tea hutOpen Saturday 8:30 am – 1 pm and on match days, with bacon rolls, coffee and squash
  • Getting hereFree parking for 60 cars, bike racks, and bus 14 stops at the gate
