Our story

It started with a borrowed book and a late return.

We met at a friend's book club in Oakland, both pretending to have finished the book. Jonah kept Amara's copy for four months. She says it was a plan; he says it was a very long novel.

  • 2020

    The book club

    One borrowed novel, many coffees, and a first date at the farmers' market.

  • 2023

    A home together

    A small flat in Temescal, a stubborn fig tree and a cat named Biscuit.

  • 2026

    The question

    Halfway up a trail in Calder Valley — the same hills where we'll say “I do.”

The day

Saturday, September 18

Everything happens in one place, outdoors until sunset and under the barn lights after. Plan to arrive by 3:30 pm.

  1. 3:30pm

    Guests arrive

    Lemonade and iced tea on the terrace. Shuttles from town drop off at the front gate.

  2. 4:00pm

    Ceremony

    On the oak lawn, about 25 minutes. It's an unplugged ceremony — phones away, please.

  3. 4:30pm

    Cocktail hour

    Drinks, small plates and a string trio while we take photos with family.

  4. 6:00pm

    Dinner & toasts

    A long-table dinner in the olive grove as the sun goes down.

  5. 8:00pm

    Dancing

    First dance, then the floor is yours in the barn. Late-night tacos at 10.

  6. 11:00pm

    Last song & shuttles

    Final shuttles leave for town at 11:15 pm and 11:45 pm.

Dress code

Garden formal

  • Suits or summer dresses; ties optional.
  • The ceremony is on grass — block heels or flats work best.
  • Hills cool down fast after sunset. Bring a layer.
  • Colour is welcome. Please leave white and ivory for the bride.

Flying in

Calder Regional (CDR) is 25 minutes away with a few daily flights. The nearest major airport is about 1 hour 20 minutes by car.

Getting around

A car is useful for the weekend; rideshare works in town but is unreliable out at the farm after dark — use the shuttle.
