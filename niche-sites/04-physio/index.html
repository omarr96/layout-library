What we treat

Four things we do really well.

We keep our focus narrow on purpose. If your problem sits outside it, we'll tell you at the first call and point you to someone better placed.

Back & neck pain

Desk-bound stiffness, a back that "went" lifting the shopping, headaches that start in the neck. Manual therapy to calm it down, then a simple routine that keeps it away.

Often seen: sciatica, disc irritation, whiplash, postural pain

Sports injuries

Runners, lifters, weekend footballers. We find out why it happened, load it back up in stages and agree a clear return-to-sport date with you.

Often seen: sprains, hamstring strains, runner's knee, shoulder pain

Post-surgery rehab

Structured programmes after knee, hip, shoulder or spinal surgery, built around your surgeon's protocol. We can start at home while you're still finding your feet.

Often seen: ACL reconstruction, joint replacement, rotator cuff repair

Women's health

Private, unhurried appointments for pregnancy, postnatal recovery and pelvic floor concerns, with a specialist physio and a door that closes.

Often seen: pelvic girdle pain, leaking, abdominal separation, return to running

Your first visit

Assessment first. Then treatment.

Before anyone starts pressing on things, we listen, test and explain. You leave the first hour knowing what's wrong, how long it should take and what you can do tonight.

What to bring: shorts or loose clothing, any scan reports or surgical notes, and your insurance card if you're claiming. Arrive five minutes early for a short health form.
  1. 1

    Listen & assess

    Your history, your goals, then movement, strength and joint tests. If anything needs a scan or a doctor, we'll say so straight away.

    About 25 minutes
  2. 2

    Explain & plan

    A plain-English diagnosis, a realistic timeline and an estimate of how many sessions you'll need. Most people need three to six.

    About 10 minutes
  3. 3

    Treat & take home

    Hands-on treatment in the same session, plus two or three exercises sent to your phone with videos, so progress doesn't wait for the next visit.

    About 25 minutes
