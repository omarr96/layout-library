Your first Sunday

What to expect, before you walk in.

Visiting somewhere new can feel like a lot. Here’s everything people usually ask us about.

  • Parking & the front door

    Park in the lot behind the building (enter from Weir Lane). Use the main doors on Mill Street — there’s a step-free ramp on the left and someone in a green lanyard to say hello.

  • About 70 minutes

    Some live music, a short talk on everyday life and faith, and time to sit quietly. Nobody will ask you to stand up, speak or sign anything.

  • Wear whatever’s comfortable

    Jeans, work boots, Sunday best — you’ll see all of it. Bring the kids, bring a friend, bring your questions.

  • Stay for coffee

    The hall stays open after both services with coffee, tea and something baked. If you’d like, we’ll introduce you to a few people — or just let you be.

Kids & youth

Somewhere they’ll actually want to go.

Every age has its own room, its own leaders and its own kind of Sunday morning.

  • 0 – 2 years

    The Nest

    A calm nursery with familiar faces. Pagers for parents, so you’re never far.

  • 3 – 10 years

    Ashby Kids

    Games, crafts, stories and snacks — grouped by age, at both services.

  • 11 – 18 years

    Youth

    Friday nights 7–9pm in the youth hall: food, music, honest conversations.

Safe by design. Secure check-in with matching name tags, two trained adults in every room, and every volunteer background-checked.

Groups & ministries

Sunday is the start. The week is where friendships happen.

Small, informal groups that meet in homes, the hall and the café down the road. Drop in any week.
