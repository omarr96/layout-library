01  What we teach

Four subjects, taught properly.

We keep the list short on purpose. Each subject is led by a teacher who has taught it in a classroom, and every plan follows your child's school curriculum.

  • i.

    Maths

    From times tables and fractions to algebra, geometry and pre-calculus. We fix the gaps first, then build speed and confidence.

    Grades 3–12
  • ii.

    English

    Reading comprehension, grammar, vocabulary and essay writing — with honest feedback on every piece your child writes.

    Grades 3–12
  • iii.

    Grades 8–12

02  How it works

You'll always know how it's going.

No guessing, no vague “doing fine”. Here's what happens from the first visit.

  1. A free trial lesson

    45 minutes with a tutor, in person or online. We gently check where your child is and what's holding them back.

  2. A plan you can read

    Within two days you get a one-page plan: three clear goals for the term and how we'll reach them.

  3. Weekly lessons, same tutor

    Your child keeps one tutor, so nothing has to be explained twice. Short homework, never piles of it.

  4. A progress note every month

    Skills, scores and a few honest lines from the tutor — by email or WhatsApp, whichever you prefer.
