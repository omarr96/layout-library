Services

Here for the big moments and the ordinary weeks

Most services are free or cost-only. Call the office or drop in after any prayer to speak to someone.

  • Madrasah & adult classes

    Children's classes Saturday and Sunday mornings, plus weekday evening courses for adults in Arabic reading, basics of faith and youth mentoring.

    From $25 a month · bursaries available

  • Nikah ceremonies

    Small ceremonies in the library room or larger ones in the hall, with an imam to officiate and help with the paperwork you'll need for a civil registration.

    Book at least 4 weeks ahead

  • Funeral services

    Washing and shrouding, the funeral prayer and liaison with the cemetery, handled with care by a trained team who will guide the family at every step.

    24-hour line: 017 00000000

  • New Muslims & the curious

    A friendly monthly circle, a mentor to call on, and an open invitation to anyone who simply wants to ask questions over tea. No pressure, ever.

    First Sunday of the month, 3pm

  • Counselling & meet an imam

    Confidential sessions with a qualified counsellor (male and female available), or a private conversation with one of our imams about family or personal matters.

    By appointment · free

Women & accessibility

Room for everyone to pray in comfort

Women have their own entrance, hall and facilities, open for every prayer, including Jumu'ah. The building was refitted in 2023 so that age or mobility is never a barrier.

  • Women's prayer hallSeparate side entrance, carpeted hall upstairs with a lift, relayed sermon on screen.
  • Wudu & toiletsDedicated women's wudu area, accessible toilet on both floors, baby-changing room.
  • Step-free throughoutRamp at the main door, lift to all floors, wide corridors and automatic doors.
  • Chairs & seated wuduPrayer chairs in every hall and a seated wudu station for those who need it.
  • Hearing loopInduction loop in the main hall; ask the office for a portable receiver.
  • Parking baysSix disabled bays by the entrance, kept free on Fridays for blue-badge holders.
