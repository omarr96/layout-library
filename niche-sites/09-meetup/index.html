What it is

Since 2022 we’ve met once a month to share what we’re building, swap what we’ve learned and put faces to names. It’s small on purpose — about eighty people, one room, no lanyards.

Who it’s for

  • Designers of every kind

    Product, brand, type, motion — or you just care a lot about how things look and work.

  • Developers who like the craft

    Front-end, back-end, mobile, hobby projects. Nobody will quiz you on frameworks.

  • People just starting out

    Students, career-changers and the newly curious. Half the room was new once — we’ll say hello.

Code of conduct

Be kind. That’s most of it.

We want everyone to feel welcome, whatever their background, experience or identity. If something feels wrong, talk to any organiser on the night or write to hello@example.com — we read every message and keep it confidential.

  • Welcome newcomersMake room in the circle. Everyone was new once.
  • Respect peopleNo harassment, put-downs or unwanted attention — in the room or online.
  • No hard sellingMention your job board or product, sure. Pitch the room, no.
  • Ask before photosLook for the green sticker — it means “happy to be in pictures”.
