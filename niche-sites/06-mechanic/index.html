How a visit works

No surprises on the bill.

The way a good local garage should work, written down so you can hold us to it.

  1. 1

    Book in a minute

    Online, by phone or on WhatsApp. Drop the car off from 7:30, take a courtesy car, or we collect it from home or work.

  2. 2

    We call before any extra work

    If we find something, you get a photo, a fixed quote and an honest view on whether it can wait. Nothing is done without your yes.

    Most services are done the same day. You get a clear invoice, old parts if you want them, and a 2-year warranty on the work.
