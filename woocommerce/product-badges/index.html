Rules

Keep the grid calm so the urgent badges still stand out.

Max 2 per card

When more apply, keep the first two in this order:

Sale→Limited→Low→New→Best→Few→Eco
Stock thresholds

10 or fewer → red "Only X left" with a pulsing dot. 11–70 → amber. Above 70 → no stock badge. 0 → grey "Sold out" and fade the image.

Only Sale is solid

Every other badge uses a soft background, so solid red always means "price drop" and matches the red strike-through price.
