FAQ

Questions?

Can't find your answer? Write to hello@wellspring.org and we'll reply within a day.

Is my donation tax deductible?
Yes. Wellspring is a registered charity in Germany, the UK and the US. You'll get a receipt by email straight away.
Can I choose which project I fund?
Yes, pick any project above. Gifts without a project go where the need is greatest.
How do I know the well was built?
Within 18 months you'll get GPS coordinates, photos and a report from the community.
Can I cancel a monthly gift?
Anytime, in one click from your donor account or the link in every receipt.
