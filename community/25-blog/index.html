About

Hi, I'm Hana

I spent ten years as a product designer working 60-hour weeks. In 2021 I burned out, quit and started writing about a better way.

Slow Work is what I've learned since: honest essays, simple tools and conversations with people who do great work at a human pace. I write from a small flat in Kyoto.
