FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is there an entrance exam?
No exam for Primary. From age 11 we use a friendly assessment and the taster day to understand how we can best support your child.
Do you offer scholarships?
Yes. Academic, music and sports scholarships of up to 50%, plus means-tested bursaries of up to 100%.
Can children join mid-year?
Yes, whenever we have space. Many families relocate mid-year and we make settling in easy.
What support is there for SEN or EAL?
Our learning support team and English-as-an-additional-language teachers work with families from day one.
