About us

Serving Leeds since 1994

First time visiting?

You're very welcome. Dress modestly, remove your shoes at the entrance, and ask any volunteer in a green lanyard for a tour.

FAQ

Questions

Can non-Muslims visit?
Of course. We run open days every month and school visits on request.
Is there parking?
We have 40 spaces, plus free street parking after 6pm. On Fridays please use the Park Lane car park.
How do I book a nikah?
Fill in the contact form below and choose "Nikah". Our imam will call you to arrange a meeting.
Do you offer help for families in need?
Yes, confidentially. Our welfare team helps with food, bills, housing advice and counselling.
