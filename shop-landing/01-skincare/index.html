Ingredient spotlight

What's inside, and why it's there

Every percentage is printed on the box. If an ingredient doesn't earn its place, it doesn't go in — most formulas have fewer than 15.

5%

Niacinamide

Vitamin B3 for the look of smaller pores and a more even-looking tone. Paired with 1% zinc PCA.

1.5%

Hyaluronic acid

Three molecular weights that hold water at different depths of the skin's surface for a plumper look.

3%

Ceramide complex

Ceramides NP, AP and EOP with cholesterol — the same lipid ratio found in healthy-looking skin.

20%

Non-nano zinc oxide

A single mineral UV filter. Broad-spectrum SPF 50, tested in an independent lab in Germany.

Always free from: added fragrance, essential oils, drying alcohols, silicones in leave-ons, and anything tested on animals.
