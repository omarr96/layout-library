Meet the beekeepers

Three generations, sixty-four hives, one kitchen table

Grandad Tom kept four hives behind the dairy in 1961. The cows are long gone, the hives now sit on three wildflower meadows, and we still label every jar at the same table.

  • Rhian Ashdown, head beekeeperChecks every hive weekly from April to September and decides when each honey is ready.
  • Mark Ashdown, fields & hensGrows the farm-box veg and looks after 240 hens in the old orchard.
  • Elsie Ashdown, candles & farm gateRolls every candle by hand and runs the shop at weekends.
