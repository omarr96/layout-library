From field to jar

Four steps, no shortcuts

We buy whole harvests from small certified-organic farms, then do the drying, blending and jarring ourselves in a converted dairy outside Utrecht.

  1. 01

    Grown in the open

    Every herb is field-grown, never under glass, by a grower we've visited in person. Certified organic.

  2. 02

    Cut by hand

    Harvested on dry mornings when the dew has lifted, and on the road to us within 24 hours.

  3. 03

    Dried low & slow

    Shade-dried on linen racks at no more than 34 °C, so colour and aroma stay in the leaf.

  4. 04

    Blended & jarred

    Weighed, blended and sealed in small batches of 40. Each jar is stamped with harvest date and farm.

Every batch is tested by an independent lab for pesticide residue and heavy metals. Certificates are available on request.
