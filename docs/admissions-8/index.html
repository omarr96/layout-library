  • Fall, Spring and Summer each year. Summer is optional, per the EEE program page.

Admission requirements

Engineering and computing programs (EEE, IPE, CoE, ME, Architecture, CSE, Data Science, CNCS):

  • SSC + HSC: total GPA of 5.00, with at least 2.50 in Science. Mathematics is required in HSC.
  • O-level: GPA of at least 2.5 in the best 5 subjects, no grade below D.
  • A-level: GPA of at least 2.0. Mathematics and Physics at grade D or above. For CoE, Computer Studies or IT also counts.
  • Only one E grade is allowed in O+A level (EEE, IPE, CoE, Architecture).
  • High School Diploma or IB: at least grade B in Mathematics. GED is not accepted.

Microbiology and Biochemistry:

  • Science group in SSC and HSC: combined 5.00, with at least 2.50 in each.
  • Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Mathematics. Without Math, an extra 3-credit course is required.

B.Pharm:

  • Total GPA 8.00, with at least 3.50 in each exam.
  • HSC: Chemistry and Biology at least 3.50; Physics and Math at least 3.00.
  • HSC must be passed this year or within the past two years.

Admission test:

  • Written exam on campus, then a viva. A sample question PDF is on the website.
  • SAT waiver: SAT ≥ 1106/1600 (or ≥ 1650/2400) skips the written test; the viva is still required.
  • Diploma holders: Not found on website.
