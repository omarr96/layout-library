How to read this

  • Every figure comes from the university's own website. Source links are at the end of each section.
  • "Not found on website" means the information was not published, or the page could not be read. It does not mean the item doesn't exist.
  • "my calculation" marks a total I worked out (credits × per-credit fee) because the university does not publish one.
  • All money is in Bangladeshi Taka (Tk), in lakh format (10,00,000 = 10 lakh). Fees change every intake, so confirm with the admission office before paying.
  • Many universities had not posted Spring 2027 dates yet; where only Fall 2026 or Summer 2026 data exists, the section says so.
